HW4 — Independent verification

This page reads week4/transactions.js directly and scans its dictionary-encoded baskets with its own counting loops. It does not load script.js and does not use countItemset, buildIndex, asIndex, the miner, or any of their cached results.

About the data being scanned: this is the exported dataset in transactions.js (541,909 raw rows cleaned to 384,911 rows, grouped by InvoiceNo into 17,080 baskets of dictionary-encoded stock indices). It is not the raw UCI workbook: original InvoiceNo values and InvoiceDate timestamps are not present, so nothing here can be keyed to time or to original invoice identifiers.
